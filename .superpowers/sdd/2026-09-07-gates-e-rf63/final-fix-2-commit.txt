fix(audit): an unclosed fence cannot hide the headings that follow it

Round two of the gates review found the fence stripping added for pasted
`# comment` lines had no unmatched-fence handling: a report with an odd
number of fence markers lost every real section heading after the stray
opener and was flagged as missing all four. audit_reports now ignores the
last fence marker when the count is odd, so an unclosed block is treated
as text and the gate fails toward flagging prose, never toward hiding a
heading. check_gates carries the case.
