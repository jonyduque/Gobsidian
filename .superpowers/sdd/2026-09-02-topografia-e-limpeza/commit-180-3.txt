docs(sugestoes): track the parser's NFD tag truncation as B20

Task 180 measured it while proving that ChaveDeTag folds case and Unicode
form: a body written in NFD, "# D\n\n#Acao\n" with A c U+0327 a U+0303 o,
comes out of the parser as n.Tags = ["Ac"], because tagNameChar
(internal/parser/ext_tag.go:36) accepts letters, digits, '-', '_' and '/'
but not unicode.Mn, so the scanner stops at the first combining mark. The
note then indexes under a tag nobody typed.

No key folding can repair a tag that arrived truncated, so this is not the
same defect Task 180 closed - and it is why that task moved its NFD fixture
to the frontmatter, where YAML preserves the form. The fix belongs to a task
of its own: changing the parser's alphabet means a golden pass over testdata/
and a parity check against Obsidian, which is what decides what a tag is.
