fix(write): note_move rewrites the moved note's own self-references at its new path

A note that cites itself by name ("[[a]]" inside a.md) enters affectedNotes
under its own pre-move key, but moverCorpo already renamed the file to its
new path by the time the rewrite loop runs. Reading and writing by the old
key gave ENOENT, leaving the move half-done (body moved, self-citation never
rewritten). caminhoAtual is now the single decision of "where this citer
lives now", used for the lock, the read and the write.
